## Brand & Style
The brand personality embodies statecraft, journalistic gravity, and political brinkmanship. Designed for political strategy enthusiasts, history scholars, and narrative gamers, the interface evokes the tension of behind-closed-doors cabinet negotiations, the intellectual weight of parliamentary decrees, and the visceral consequence of executive governance under the motto: *"El poder se conquista. Gobernar es otra historia."*

The visual language fuses **Editorial Journalism** and **Tactile Bureaucracy** with cinematic dark strategy interfaces. It contrasts deep charcoal cartographic War Rooms with archival parchment dossiers, classified memorandums, and parliamentary voting chambers. The mood is dignified, deliberate, and high-stakes—avoiding arcade tropes in favor of presidential seals, departmental cartography, legislative hemicicles, and solemn newsprint aesthetics optimized for one-thumb mobile governance.

## Colors
The palette balances the deep atmospheric darkness of executive strategy with the archival warmth of executive desks and historic statecraft:

- **Primary (`#E87532`)**: Presidential Amber/Orange. Used for supreme executive actions, active vetoes, critical power decisions, and focal interactive states.
- **Secondary (`#F3EBDD`)**: Archival Cream / Papyrus. Reserved for ministerial briefs, official gazettes, decrypted telegrams, and high-legibility state documents.
- **Tertiary (`#557568`)**: Sage Patina. Conveys fiscal surplus, institutional stability, diplomatic treaties, and social calm.
- **Neutral Deep Charcoal (`#252A2B` base with `#181C1D` core)**: Forms the war-room substrate, tactical command viewports, and systemic backdrop.
- **Factional Accents**: Applied strictly to party indicators, coalition bars, and parliamentary seats:
  - *Frente Amplio*: `#2E5B9A` (Azure) / `#C7362E` (Tricolor Red)
  - *Partido Nacional*: `#4B9CD3` (Patriotic Sky Blue) / `#FFFFFF` (White)
  - *Partido Colorado*: `#D02B2B` (Batllista Scarlet)
  - *Cabildo Abierto*: `#606834` (Olive Gold)
  - *Partido Independiente*: `#734F96` (Civic Violet)

## Typography
The typographic hierarchy creates a sharp intellectual contrast between the moral weight of governance and ruthless data analytics:

- **Headlines (Newsreader)**: Evokes the solemn weight of the Diario Oficial, historic constitution texts, and breaking editorial headlines. Used for bill titles, national crises, newspaper leads, and presidential decrees.
- **Body (Inter)**: Clean, neutral, high-legibility interface typography engineered for dense legal summaries, constituent briefings, and ministerial dialogues on mobile screens.
- **Labels & Tactical Telemetry (JetBrains Mono)**: Represents national budgets, macro-indicators (inflation, approval ratings, treasury reserves), parliamentary seat counts, and encrypted intelligence timestamps.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout adheres to a 4-column fluid system on mobile screens that broadens to 8 columns on tablets and 12 columns on desktop command dashboards.

- **Mobile First Ergonomics**: All actionable buttons, legislative decrees, and emergency response selections stay anchored inside the bottom 40% of the viewport for comfortable one-handed thumb interaction. Top viewports house the national ledger bar (Treasury, Public Approval, Coalition Cohesion, International Confidence).
- **Responsive Adaptations**:
  - **Phone (<640px)**: Single-column modal dossiers overlaying full-screen cartographic views. Dossiers slide up from the bottom sheet.
  - **Tablet (641px–1024px)**: Dual pane; interactive 19-department map on the left, executive desk and voting hemicicle on the right.
  - **Desktop (>1024px)**: Full ministerial War Room with persistent live senate hemicicle, macro-economic graphs, and classified drawer.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation is realized through archival layering and tonal hierarchy rather than artificial drop shadows:

- **Base Layer (Level 0 - Tactical Deep `#181C1D`)**: Vector department borders of Uruguay, hydrographic basins, road grids, and regional tension indicators.
- **Surface Layer (Level 1 - Executive Slate `#252A2B`)**: Fixed structural UI, navigation docks, and ministerial metrics boards framed with subtle 1px low-contrast brass borders (`rgba(232, 117, 50, 0.15)`).
- **Raised Dossier Layer (Level 2 - Editorial Parchment `#F3EBDD`)**: Critical dilemma cards, presidential decrees, and urgent press clippings. These cast a diffused, tinted ambient silhouette (`rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.45)` with 24px blur) creating physical presence over the map.
- **Top Tier (Level 3 - State Seal & Overlays)**: Emergency sessions, constitutional vetoes, and irreversible choice modals framed in deep obsidian with glowing amber accents.

## Shapes
A structured, geometric shape language reflecting bureaucratic formality:
- Base elements (buttons, ledger cards, data cells) use a strict `0.25rem` (4px) soft corner radius, retaining the rigid feel of paper stock, stamp plates, and governmental filing systems.
- Archival dossier cards use `0.5rem` (`rounded-lg`) to emulate bound legal folders.
- Hemicicle seat nodes and round ministerial status badges are circular pills, creating distinct tactical contrast against geometric text blocks.

## Components

### Buttons & Presidential Directives
- **Primary Executive Action**: Deep `#E87532` background with `#181C1D` bold text, a sharp 1px `#F3EBDD` hairline inner border, and crisp typography. Emits an amber rim glow on active touch.
- **Secondary Veto / Dismiss**: `#252A2B` background with 1px border of `#557568` or parchment tone.
- **Irreversible Decree**: High-contrast black cardstock accented with dual-line guilloche patterning and national seal watermarks.

### Dilemma & Dossier Cards
- Constructed with `#F3EBDD` papyrus background and `#181C1D` text. Features a distressed editorial headline in *Newsreader*, accompanied by an archival ministerial seal stamp, leak classification tags (`CONFIDENCIAL`, `URGENTE`), and clear ideological/fiscal impact projections displayed in *JetBrains Mono*.

### Parliamentary Hemicicle
- An interactive radial arc mapping 99 Representatives and 30 Senators. Seats are micro-orbs colored dynamically according to the five political parties. Tapping a coalition party filters their voting disposition, party discipline index, and current loyalty score.

### Departmental Map Nodes (19 Departamentos)
- Vectorized polygons styled in deep monochrome slate (`#252A2B`). Hovering or tapping activates border glows in `#E87532`, unfolding regional unrest, electoral polling, and infrastructure status.

### Metric Gauges & Status Strips
- Telemetry modules featuring razor-thin progress tracks (`2px`), mono-spaced readouts, and sage green (`#557568`) trends for positive stability or scarlet batllista for rising inflation/strike risks.

### Newspaper Alerts
- Breaking press clippings with traditional multi-column serif layout, halftone printed photography filters, drop caps, and journalistic cynicism reacting directly to player enactments.
